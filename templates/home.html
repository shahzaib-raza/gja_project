About GJA

Established in Singapore in 2026, GJA is a member-led association for the gems, jewellery and watch trade. We bring together industry professionals through networking, education, business exposure and shared initiatives that support long-term trade growth.

Mission & Vision

Guiding GJA’s role as a platform for member support, industry connection and long-term trade growth.

M

Our Mission

To support and connect businesses across the gems, jewellery and watch trade by creating opportunities for visibility, collaboration, education and shared growth.

Through member-focused initiatives, networking platforms and industry programmes, GJA aims to help businesses strengthen their presence, build meaningful relationships and remain active within the trade community.

V

Our Vision

To establish GJA as a trusted voice and platform for Singapore’s gems, jewellery and watch community, promoting professionalism, unity and long-term industry growth.

We envision a stronger and more connected industry where members can collaborate with confidence, uphold good trade practices and create lasting value for businesses, professionals and the wider market.
